Security-first by design

Your keys. Armored.

The wallet stack is built like infrastructure, not a demo: envelope encryption, scoped sessions and a tamper-evident audit trail on every lamport that moves.

🔐 Envelope encryption at rest

Private keys are sealed with AES-256-GCM under a rotating master key. They exist in plaintext only in memory, only for the milliseconds a signature takes — then wiped.

📌 PIN-gated sessions

Swaps, withdrawals and exports require your argon2id-hashed PIN. Five wrong attempts lock the wallet for 15 minutes; unlocked sessions are scoped and expire just as fast.

⏳ Self-destructing key export

Need your key out? The export message hides behind a spoiler and auto-deletes after 30 seconds — with the event written to the audit log.

🧾 Tamper-evident audit chain

Every balance change is idempotent, compare-and-swap transitioned and hash-chained into an append-only audit log. Money can't move twice, and history can't be rewritten.

Questions

Asked often.

Is Flash SolanaSwap custodial?

Yes — the bot holds a wallet on your behalf so trades execute instantly from chat. Keys are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM under a rotating master key, gated by your argon2id PIN, and you can export your private key at any time (the reveal self-destructs after 30 seconds). Treat it like a hot wallet: keep trading balances, not your life savings.

What does a swap cost?

A flat 0.5% platform fee per swap, plus the usual Solana network fee. Quotes are locked for 15 seconds and show the exact receive amount before you confirm — no hidden spreads.

Which tokens can I trade?

Any token routable through Jupiter on Solana mainnet — SOL, USDC, BONK, JUP and the long tail of SPL tokens. The bot quotes the best route across all major Solana DEXs.

How do limit orders and TP/SL work?

You set a target rate (limit) or USD trigger price (TP/SL). Background engines poll the market every 15 seconds and fill exactly once — limit orders live for 7 days, TP/SL watches around the clock.

How does the referral program pay?

Share your personal link (t.me/FlashSolanaSwapBot?start=ref_CODE). You earn 25% of the platform fee on every swap your referrals make, accrued automatically and paid out in SOL once you cross 0.01 SOL.
